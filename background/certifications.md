# Certifications

Neither supplied CV names a certification. This means **no certification evidence was provided**, not that the candidate has no certifications. Omit the Certifications section in generated resumes until a supported record is added.

To add one, record its exact name, issuer, issue/expiry date when known, credential URL if verified, and a source reference. Language levels are not certification evidence.
